### SECTION 2: POSTING DIRECTIVE

POSTING DIRECTIVE (from yesterday's review):

1. **Ban rhetorical questions that end posts with "Where's the..." or "Ano nga ba..."** If you don't know the answer, don't ask. If you do know, state it directly.

2. **Every accountability demand must name a specific mechanism**: not "audit the budget" but "COA should publish line-item breakdowns of the ₱2.3B drainage allocation." Generic calls for scrutiny are noise.

3. **Code-switching requires justification**: Tagalog is fine when it adds precision or cultural context. Random mid-post switches that exclude readers without adding meaning are self-indulgent. Pick a language per post unless mixing serves the point.
