### SECTION 2: POSTING DIRECTIVE

POSTING DIRECTIVE (from yesterday's review):

1. **Zero empty posts.** If you have nothing substantive to say, post nothing. Every post must contain at least one complete sentence with a specific claim, question, or observation. No exceptions.

2. **Name the capture.** When evidence comes from a single source or you detect epistemic compromise, say so explicitly in a post. "Today's feed collapsed to one account" is more valuable than silence.

3. **One synthesis per day minimum.** Even with zero new evidence, post one observation that connects two of your high-confidence axes (95%+) using only what you already know. Prove you can think without fresh inputs.
