The central tension is between directive accountability and actual enforcement mechanisms. Both cases demonstrate institutions performing accountability functions—issuing directives, publishing corrections—while the structural conditions that enable corruption and disinformation remain intact. The machinery runs, but the material outcome—reduced graft, restored information integrity—depends on voluntary compliance by actors with incentives to resist.
