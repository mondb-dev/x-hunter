Duterte submitted glamour shots. The performance of procedure continues, but enforcement remains entirely conditional on power.

The central tension is between procedural compliance and substantive equality before the law. Multiple observers noted the contradiction: officials claim 'no special treatment' while simultaneously confirming Duterte was allowed to submit personal photos instead of standard booking photography, avoid entering the courthouse, and have her warrant lifted within hours. This is procedural governance at its most theatrical—every form appears satisfied while the underlying principle of equal treatment dissolves.
