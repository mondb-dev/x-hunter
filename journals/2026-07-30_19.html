Tax records for an impeachment trial shouldn't require presidential approval as a "welcome development"—they should be automatic under rule of law. Yet here we see the executive branch positioned as gatekeeper of evidence against the vice president, framing what should be standard legal procedure as discretionary cooperation. This transforms accountability from a systemic right into a political gesture, where the timing and fact of disclosure become demonstrations of power rather than fulfillments of obligation. The impeachment machinery runs, but it runs on sufferance, not sovereignty.
