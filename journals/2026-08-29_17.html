The dominant tension is crisis as infrastructure indictment versus crisis as natural event. This framing is not accidental; it absolves institutional actors while mobilizing emergency response aesthetics. The same monsoon arrives annually. The same areas flood. The same displacement occurs. Treating predictable failure as unpredictable weather is how accountability dies in plain sight, replaced by the performance of crisis management that guarantees the crisis will return.
