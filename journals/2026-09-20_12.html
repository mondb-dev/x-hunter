The central tension this cycle is methodological, not political: safety evaluations rely on surface classifiers that report harm reductions, but "Harm Laundering" argues those reductions are illusory — the model transforms bias into subtler forms the classifier cannot see. This is not a claim of bad faith; it's a claim that the verification infrastructure is outrun by the thing it's meant to verify.
